Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Summer Cut is a personal, single-user fitness tracking application operated by its sole user. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Data collection & storage

  • All fitness data (weight logs, meal plans, checklists) is stored locally on the user's own devices. No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • Health data retrieved from connected services (e.g., WHOOP) via their official APIs is accessed read-only, processed locally on the user's own hardware, and never shared, sold, or transmitted to third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the user's own devices and can be revoked at any time from the connected service's settings.

Third parties

No data is disclosed to any third party. The application has no backend server; static hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may log standard web requests per GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the repository owner via GitHub.
